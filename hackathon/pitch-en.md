# London Civic Hack 2026: five free apps, ready to pilot

**What we did.** Six research tracks (media and complaints, visitors, surveys, audiences and ages, company reports and policy, civic apps worldwide) produced 72 evidence-based ideas. Judges weighted value to residents (25%), value to the city (20%), the gap filled (20%), feasibility (15%), privacy and zero running cost (10%) and reach (10%). The winner of each track became a working prototype.

**Shared principles.** Free forever, no accounts, no tracking, works offline, plain English, WCAG 2.2 AA, open code and open data (TfL Unified API, police.uk, OpenStreetMap, Open-Meteo, postcodes.io). Each app adds to official services. None replaces them.

| App | The problem (evidence) | What it does | Who should own or partner | Pilot metric (action, not downloads) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Report It Right** | London has 32 boroughs + the City, TfL, the Met, BTP, NHS and utilities, and no single "311". 4,593 VAWG offences on the TfL network in 2025, about 3% charged. | 20 everyday situations → the right channel first time; a 61016 text builder for BTP; borough finder; quick exit for domestic abuse; reports kept on the phone only. | GLA / LOTI, Met, BTP, boroughs | Share of reports that reach the right channel first time; 61016 messages started from the app |
| **PhoneGuard London** | 71,391 phone thefts and robberies in 2025 (+54% since 2022); about 2.4% of stolen phones recovered. | Harden the phone before going out (iOS/Android), live police.uk hotspots without stigma labels, a 15-minute response plan in 6 languages. | MOPAC, Met (Phone Theft Command Cell), West End BIDs | Checklist completion; time from theft to SIM block (survey of victims) |
| **London Ready** | Official UK ETA costs £20; unofficial sites charge up to $99–€178. Airport transfer price traps; contactless confusion. | ETA "official or not" checker, airport-to-centre options with traps flagged, contactless coach, service-charge calculator, emergency card in 6 languages. | Home Office (ETA comms), TfL, London & Partners, VisitBritain | Scam links caught; visitors choosing contactless over Visitor Oyster |
| **Step-Free & Calm** | Only 94 of 272 Tube stations are step-free; long lift outages; disabled Londoners make far fewer trips. | Live lift status, Plan B to the nearest step-free stations on the same line, busyness now vs usual, quiet-time heatmap, offline station directory. | TfL accessibility and open data teams, Transport for All | Journeys re-planned via Plan B; off-peak shifts suggested and taken |
| **Free London Today** | 26% of Londoners live in poverty after housing costs; 31% of 16–29s report loneliness; 97 public toilets closed in a decade. | 78 free places open right now, weather-aware ranking, free half-day plans, nearby water, toilets and libraries. | GLA culture and cost-of-living teams, London & Partners, Age UK London | Visits to free venues beyond Zone 1 started from the app |

**Before any pilot.** Each app's README lists what must be checked first (phone numbers, fares, settings menus, translations, live API shapes). The research relied on search results, so the cited figures carry confidence marks and should be checked against their primary sources.
