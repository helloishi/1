About Report It Right

London has no single number for reporting problems. Services are split across 32 boroughs and the City of London, TfL, the Met, British Transport Police, City of London Police, the NHS and utilities. Report It Right sends you to the right one first time.

The principle: no wrong door

Whatever you choose, you get one clear next step, other ways to report, what to include, and support. You should never need to know who is responsible first.

Why this matters

4,593 violence against women and girls offences were recorded on the TfL network in 2025, and about 3% led to a charge (London Assembly figures reported in 2026, medium confidence). British Transport Police's discreet text service, 61016, exists, but under-reporting is high.

Fewer people should give up because they do not know where to go. Better routing can mean fewer misdirected 999 and 101 calls, more reports of under-reported crime and better data. It also works alongside FixMyStreet.

It works elsewhere

  • Singapore OneService: over 620,000 users and 2.5 million+ reports (MND Singapore, 2024).
  • NYC311: 36.5 million contacts in 2025, 2.6 million through the app (NYC Mayor's Management Report 2026).
  • UK FixMyStreet: over 1.1 million reports in 2025 (SocietyWorks).

London context from the hackathon research synthesis.

Who it helps

Everyone. Especially women and girls, older people facing scams, LGBTQ+ and faith communities facing hate incidents, newcomers, and people who do not want to phone.

Privacy

No accounts, no analytics, no cookies, no trackers. Nothing leaves your phone except the postcode lookup, and only when you ask for it. Your saved reports stay on this device. If you share this phone, you can delete them in My reports.

The optional "Ask Claude to suggest" button only appears where Claude is available. It sends your description when you press it.
