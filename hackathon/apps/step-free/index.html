Calm-travel tips

  • Travel off-peak when you can.
  • Every Elizabeth line and DLR station is step-free.
  • TfL's free "Please offer me a seat" badge and the Travel Support Card let you show staff what help you need.
  • The Sunflower lanyard signals a hidden disability and is widely recognised on UK transport.

Help

Turn-up-and-go assistance

Ask any member of staff. They can help you get on and off trains. You do not need to book.

Tell staff about a broken lift

Tell a member of staff at the gateline. They can report it and help you plan another step-free route. TfL may arrange other transport if a lift is out.

How the data works, and its limits

  • Lift and crowding information comes live from TfL's open data, straight to your phone.
  • A lift can break between TfL updates. "No lift problems reported" is not a guarantee.
  • Busyness shows TfL's typical level at the station gates, not seats on trains.
  • Plan B lists nearby stations on the same lines. It is not a checked route.
  • When live data is not available, the app says so. It never shows made-up numbers.
